# Getting help

## Where to ask

- **A problem or a question:** [open an issue](https://github.com/typmaxdev/typmax/issues/new/choose) and pick Bug report or Question.
- **An idea or a feature you want:** pick **Feature request**.
- **A security problem:** never in an issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Questions are welcome — you don't need to be sure something is a bug. If you like, have a quick look through [existing issues](https://github.com/typmaxdev/typmax/issues?q=is%3Aissue) too.

## What to include

- The version and build (Help › About).
- Your browser or OS.
- What you did, step by step, what happened, and what you expected.
- If the editor showed an error, press **Copy error details** and paste it in. It never holds your board.

## What never to attach

Issues here are public. Never attach or paste:

- a board, schematic or project file, unless you mean to make it public (if we need one, we will ask, and you choose);
- passwords, tokens, licence keys or other secrets;
- other people's names, addresses or other personal details.

If you attached something by mistake, edit the issue to remove it, and tell us so we can delete old versions of the comment.
